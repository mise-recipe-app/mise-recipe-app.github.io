Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what the Mise app (“Mise”, “the app”) does with your information.

What stays on your device

Your recipes, photos, meal plans and shopping lists are stored only on your phone. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not run a server that receives your recipes or lists. We cannot see them.

Information you choose to share

If you tap Share on a recipe or a shopping list, the app hands that text to the sharing menu of your phone, and it goes wherever you send it. We do not receive it. If you import a recipe or list, the app reads only the text you paste.

Photos

If you add a photo to a recipe, the app asks for access to your camera or photo library. Photos you add are copied into the app’s storage on your device and are never uploaded by us.

Advertising

Mise is free and shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with ads. This is handled by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks for your permission before tracking you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised using that tracking. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and similar regions: the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice later.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Settings → Privacy → Ads.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not receive the data Google collects about you.

Children

Mise is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
