Mise

Your recipes, meal plan and shopping list in one place.

Mise is a free recipe app. Save the recipes you cook, add photos, plan what to make over the next few days, and turn the plan into a shopping list with quantities already combined. Everything is stored on your phone and works without a connection.

What it does

  • Write down recipes with ingredients, steps, photos and tags
  • Plan meals for the week and send them to a shopping list
  • Share a recipe or a shopping list as text, and import ones sent to you
